Executive Summary

In January, we couldn't spend as much as we wanted due to card issues leading to fund addition problems, for which the Meta ad account got disabled post 21st January 2026. However, the Google Search campaign continued running with a very small daily budget of around ₹500. We ended the month with approximately ₹23k revenue and a ROAS of 0.68. This operational disruption significantly impacted our ability to execute planned marketing strategies and limited our growth potential for the month.

Platform Performance Analysis

M Meta (Facebook & Instagram)
  • Initially we saw a good performance coming in and we were aiming to get to a position where we get to receiving an order a day
  • We tested 4 audiences simultaneously while we started receiving orders from them, still we couldn't end up considering those audiences as winners as that was too early to judge and also we didn't hit our benchmarks with those
  • Many of the optimisations outside ads were also recommended to improve the conversion rate
G Google Search Campaigns
  • Here we're running only the search campaign with a daily budget of ₹500 only. As the daily budget was low initially, and we hadn't got a green signal to start spending 20k by 8th of February at that time. So we only focused on one product to promote through search ads i.e. Omegaco
  • Here we didn't get any qualified traction along with no conversion rate
  • Though our ads started ranking good, but we lacked trustability factor as a new brand in the market, and people searching for the product usually are high intent customers who are looking for very specific products and that too choose specific known brands especially in case of products related to health care
