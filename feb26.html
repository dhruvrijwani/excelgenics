🟢 February Baseline Established

February is Excelgenics' foundational performance month. With a 1.22x ROAS, campaigns are in the learning phase — spend is being deployed, audiences are being tested, and conversion pathways are being refined. The priority for March is to push ROAS toward 1.8x–2.0x by improving creative performance, audience segmentation, and AOV.

🧬
Category: Nutraceuticals
Nutraceutical brands typically require a longer consideration window. Trust-building creatives and educational content are critical for conversion rate improvement.
🎯
Avg. Order Value Focus
Current AOV is ~₹1,681. Subscription offers, bundles, and combo packs can be powerful levers to lift AOV and ROAS simultaneously.
📍
Learning Phase Active
Ad algorithms are in active learning. Frequent budget changes should be avoided — allow campaigns to exit the learning phase before major optimization decisions.
🚚 RTO Impact — Net Revenue Adjustment

Additionally, in February we recorded RTOs of approximately ₹36,000. After adjusting the total revenue by excluding these RTOs, the net revenue as of data received till 5th March stands at ₹91,300, resulting in an ROI of 0.87x.

🔍
Search Campaign — OmegaCo Focus
On Google, we initially tested Search campaigns, primarily focusing on OmegaCo, as Omega supplements generally have strong market demand and are widely recognized products.
🚫
No Conversions Despite Rankings
Despite achieving top search rankings, the campaigns did not generate conversions. While users were clicking to explore the product, the lack of established brand trust — common in the nutraceutical category — impacted purchase decisions.
🧪
PMax Tested — No Traction
We also tested Performance Max (PMax) campaigns with the objective of driving orders. However, even after experimenting with multiple audience signals, the campaigns did not generate meaningful traction.
🔀
Budget Shifted to Meta
Based on early performance, we decided to shift our focus and allocate the remaining budget to Meta, where we were already seeing better engagement and order potential — allowing us to scale what was working instead of testing across multiple platforms simultaneously.
🔄 Google Reintroduction Plan

We can reintroduce Google campaigns once the brand gains stronger traction, and we begin seeing more stable ROAS and revenue growth from the current Meta campaigns.

💡 Google Ads Credit Strategy

Additionally, the Google spend was primarily utilized to invest the initial ₹20k in order to unlock the additional ₹20k Google Ads credit by 8th February, while also maximizing platform learnings through sales objective campaigns within that budget.

📊 Projection Basis

While our primary priority will be to achieve a minimum revenue target of ₹1 lakh for the month, this projection is based on implementing the suggested strategies along with aggressive creative testing across different marketing angles, particularly through video creatives. This approach will help us aim for a 1x+ improvement in ROAS.

Notes for March
  • 01
    Revenue Target — ₹1 Lakh at 1x ROAS Our target for March is to achieve ₹1 lakh in revenue with a 1x ROAS.
  • 02
    Focus on Scaling Top Performers The primary strategy will be to focus on scaling products and campaigns that are already performing well and generating higher traction, rather than distributing the budget across all products. Expanding to other products can be considered with a separate dedicated testing budget.
  • 03
    Budget Discipline — Stay Within ₹1 Lakh We will ensure that the overall ad spend remains within the ₹1 lakh budget for the month.
  • 04
    Timely Fund Addition — Critical for Continuity Excelgenics should ensure timely addition of funds to avoid any interruptions, as disruptions in budget flow can affect campaign learning and performance stability.
  • 05
    Magneva & CoQHealth — Dual Focus While Magneva continues to generate the highest number of orders, we are also focusing on pushing CoQHealth, as it is showing promising traction and potential to scale with a stronger ROAS.
